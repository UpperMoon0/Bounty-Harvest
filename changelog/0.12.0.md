# Bounty Harvest 0.12.0

## 15-level farm economy overhaul

- Rebuilt Levels 1-8 around production chains instead of vanilla checklist filler.
- Added Levels 9-15 with Corn Delight, Create, Pineapple Delight, Slice & Dice, Create Crafts & Additions, Alex's Delight, Alex's Caves, Twilight Forest, and Twilight's Flavors & Delight progression.
- Added `PROGRESSION_DESIGN.md` as the design contract for future progression work: old production must keep gaining value, new levels should multiply existing systems, crop introductions should be paced, technology tiers must have room to breathe, and automation should be justified by rising throughput.
- Expanded copper into a full Level 7-9 technology era. Completing the Better Copper toolset proves copper mastery but no longer grants Ironworking.
- Moved the single Ironworking unlock to the Level 10 promotion. Advancing into iron now consumes copper and charcoal from the mature pre-iron economy before exposing iron ore, ingots, buckets, tools, armor, and iron-dependent kitchen equipment.
- Reworked Level 7 around workshop supplies so charcoal, leather/paper/bookshelves, wool/yarn/sweaters, wood, and copper all return as useful production lines instead of becoming dead inventory.
- Staggered Farmer's Delight crops instead of introducing four crops at once: cabbage at Level 8, tomato at Level 9, onion at Level 10, and rice at Level 11.
- Rebuilt Level 8 as a pre-iron Market Garden & Hearth tier. Cabbage combines with older chicken, fish, potatoes, carrots, apples, wheat dough, eggs, and sugar through prepared foods before iron exists; required recipes now use Cutting Board outputs instead of bypassing the processing tier.
- Reworked Level 9 tacos into a cross-level product using corn/tortilla, cooked pork, tomato, and cabbage so the new crop economy increases the value of older animal and garden production.
- Added expensive deterministic recovery trades for Corn Delight corn seeds and Pineapple Delight pineapple crops so unlucky biome/world-generation rolls cannot hard-lock progression.
- Reworked Level 10 into Iron & Industry: Ironworking, onion, the iron kitchen, Create foundations, and redstone now arrive together as the first major industrial breakthrough.
- Delayed rice to Level 11 so it immediately supports pineapple fried rice and reconnects the old fisheries economy through salmon rolls; the level now requires a Slice & Dice Slicer and industrial-volume prepared-food production.
- Rebuilt Level 12 Power & Logistics so motors, alternators, and accumulators remain player infrastructure while the Market Order tests the bulk farm output that infrastructure enables.
- Reworked Levels 14-15 so Cave Tablets and Magic Maps remain reusable expedition/navigation infrastructure instead of disposable Market Order goods; expeditions now reconnect to large farm-provisioning shipments.
- Removed motors, alternators, accumulators, Cave Tablets, and Magic Maps from repeatable Bountiful objectives so enabling infrastructure stays with the player and repeatable market pressure falls on renewable output.
- Removed the unused Level 15 End's Delight namespace gate until the integration has a real progression branch.
- Moved fisheries to Level 5 and restored the vanilla fishing-rod recipe so the tier is reachable before Ironworking.
- Integrated Aquaculture 2 and an actually early Ocean's Delight recipe instead of requiring late kitchen ingredients.
- Reworked every level-up into a market promotion paid with goods and the currency available from the preceding bounty tier. Industrial-era promotion currency rises approximately by doubling: 48 iron at Level 10, 96 at Level 11, 192 at Level 12, then 48/96/192 gold across Levels 13-15.
- Added a Market Order milestone to every level; later contracts intentionally reuse earlier crops, animal goods, foods, textiles, fuel, and machinery. Late-game Market Orders now demand hundreds of renewable outputs, culminating in 512-unit prepared-food lines at Level 15.
- Rebuilt the repeatable decree market for Levels 1-15 with denomination-appropriate prices and a geometric renewable-demand curve after Create: 4x at Level 10, 8x at Level 11, 16x at Level 12, 32x at Level 13, 64x at Level 14, and 128x at Level 15. Rare maps, tablets, and enabling machines are excluded from repeatable objectives rather than merely exempted from scaling.
- Raised Bountiful reward caps so industrial-volume orders can still pay proportionally instead of being capped at a few coins.
- Added readable high-priority KubeJS Bountiful data for all 15 decree/objective pools and copper/iron/gold reward tiers, including stronger processed reuse for chicken, fish, corn, rice, charcoal, leather, and textiles.
- Redistributed ItemStages gates across all 15 levels and removed the misleading Alex's Mobs namespace gate; entities are not represented as item-gated systems.
- Moved redstone to Level 10, gold to the electrical tier, and diamond/lapis to the deep-expedition tier.
- Extended quest/pack validation from 8 to 15 levels, including the market-order spine, generated bounty data, namespace policy, reachability, cycles, delayed Level 10 Ironworking, crop pacing, quest descriptions, major old-production reuse invariants, deterministic crop recovery, retained infrastructure, and the automation-economy growth curve.
