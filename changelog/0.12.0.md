# Bounty Harvest 0.12.0

## Progression overhaul

- Rebuilt the pack around a **23-level farm-management economy** instead of a mostly linear vanilla checklist.
- Added `PROGRESSION_DESIGN.md` as the design contract for pacing, reuse, automation, staging, quest structure, and validation.
- Every main level ends in a one-time Market Order.
- Core quest branches feed the Market Order; optional branches reconnect through post-market Mastery nodes instead of ending as disconnected spokes.
- Added graph validation for unique IDs, reachability, cycles, core-to-market connectivity, optional-to-Mastery connectivity, and excessive promotion fan-out.

## Quest-book readability

- Fixed FTB Quests `Invalid formatting` errors caused by natural-language ampersands in displayed quest text.
- Added `tools/format_quest_text.py` to normalize prose throughout the quest book.
- Distinct ideas use explicit blank paragraph separators and oversized prose is split at sentence boundaries.
- CI rejects unformatted quest text and missing descriptions.

## 23-level pacing

The expanded progression prevents several unrelated systems from being dumped into the same tier:

- L1 Homestead and Orders
- L2 Poultry and Hearth
- L3 Cattle and Leather
- L4 Roots and Pork
- L5 Fisheries and Coast
- L6 Wool and Textiles
- L7 Potatoes and Pantry
- L8 Sugar and Baking
- L9 Copper Workshop
- L10 Cabbage and Butchery
- L11 Corn Economy
- L12 Tomato and Tacos
- L13 Ironworking
- L14 Onion and Iron Kitchen
- L15 Mechanical Farming with Create
- L16 Pineapple Plantation
- L17 Rice and Slice and Dice
- L18 Power Generation
- L19 Electric Drive and Storage
- L20 Wildlife Cuisine
- L21 Deep Expeditions
- L22 Twilight Frontier
- L23 End Frontier

Copper now has a real era from L9 through L12. Ironworking unlocks at L13. Create does not arrive until L15.

## Processing and reuse

- The Cutting Board and flint knife are real L10 infrastructure; required chicken, fish, and beef recipes use processed outputs rather than whole-meat shortcuts.
- Corn and tomato are separated into L11 and L12 instead of arriving together.
- Pineapple and rice are separated into L16 and L17.
- Slice and Dice is required infrastructure at L17 and feeds salmon-roll and pineapple-fried-rice production.
- Old wheat, livestock, fish, sugar, corn, cabbage, tacos, pies, sandwiches, leather, and other established chains repeatedly return in later recipes and market demand.
- Corn and pineapple retain expensive deterministic recovery routes so world-generation luck cannot hard-lock progression.

## Automation economy

- L14 is the last intentionally hand-manageable tier.
- Create arrives at L15 and renewable Bountiful demand then doubles every level:
  - L15 4x
  - L16 8x
  - L17 16x
  - L18 32x
  - L19 64x
  - L20 128x
  - L21 256x
  - L22 512x
  - L23 1024x
- Newly introduced frontier goods may start at fixed quantities rather than inheriting the full legacy multiplier immediately. Glowstew therefore begins at a fixed L22 repeatable volume, while mature L22 staples carry the 512x pressure.
- Chorus-fruit pies are part of the final L23 renewable economy and receive the 1024x repeatable scale.
- Rare ores, navigation objects, expedition keys, and enabling machines are not multiplied into absurd recurring quantities.

## Promotion costs

- Industrial iron-coin promotion costs continue through L15-L18 at 192 / 384 / 768 / 1536.
- The switch to gold no longer resets difficulty. L19-L23 continue at 384 / 768 / 1536 / 3072 / 6144 gold coins, alongside large previous-economy goods.

## Infrastructure retention

- L18 establishes generation with an alternator.
- L19 adds electric drive and storage with an electric motor and accumulator.
- These machines remain player infrastructure rather than Market Order sacrifices.
- The Cave Tablet remains expedition infrastructure at L21.
- The Twilight Magic Map remains navigation infrastructure at L22.
- Repeatable Bountiful objectives exclude permanent enabling objects.

## Frontier integrations

- L20 establishes Alex's Delight wildlife cuisine without replacing mature farm demand.
- L21 combines Alex's Caves exploration with industrial provisioning.
- L22 separates Twilight navigation from Twilight cuisine.
- L23 separates chorus farming from End's Delight processing; chorus-fruit pie reconnects the End to old wheat, sugar, and pie-crust production.

## Bounty Board decree fix

- Confirmed that Bountiful 6.0.4 initializes a pristine board with a blank decree that can resolve to a random loaded decree.
- Added pack-side placement logic that seeds a newly crafted Bounty Board with the placing player's highest unlocked `level_N` decree before Bountiful's first population tick.
- Broken and re-placed boards preserve their saved decree/bounty state.

## CI and release safety

- Feature branches no longer duplicate validation through both `push` and `pull_request` events.
- Added concurrency groups with `cancel-in-progress` so superseded PR runs stop automatically.
- The expensive Windows archive/server job is gated behind cheap Ubuntu progression/runtime validation.
- Server smoke has a longer timeout and always preserves diagnostic logs on failure.
- Hardened server checks reject KubeJS runtime/data errors and unknown-item recipe failures even when Forge still reaches `Done`.
