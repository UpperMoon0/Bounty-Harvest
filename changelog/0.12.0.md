# Bounty Harvest 0.12.0

## Progression overhaul

- Rebuilt the pack around a 15-level farm-management economy instead of a mostly linear vanilla checklist.
- Added `PROGRESSION_DESIGN.md` as the design contract for progression, reuse, automation, staging, and validation.
- Every main level now ends in a one-time Market Order that consumes production output and unlocks the next promotion.
- Main chapters are deliberately branched: crops, animals, processing, technology, exploration, and support systems appear as separate quest paths where relevant, then converge on the Market Order.
- Restored and reorganized useful detail from the older Level 1-6 progression instead of compressing each level into a three-node promotion/production/order line.
- Added structural validation so a level cannot silently collapse back into a straight-line quest chain.

## Quest-book readability

- Fixed FTB Quests `Invalid formatting` errors caused by natural-language ampersands in displayed quest text, including the remaining `Research & Levels` chapter title.
- Added `tools/format_quest_text.py` to normalize quest prose across the entire quest book.
- Distinct description ideas now receive explicit blank paragraph separators instead of rendering back-to-back as one wall of text.
- Oversized single paragraphs are split at natural sentence boundaries for the compact FTB Quests description window.
- CI now verifies that committed quest text is already normalized and rejects unsafe or unreadable quest-text regressions.

## Early game and farming

- Level 1 now requires the Bounty Board and verifies that the player completed a first Bountiful sale before progression starts charging promotion currency.
- Restored separate homestead, storage, tool, animal, material, cooking, textile, fishing, and pantry branches through the early levels.
- Added deterministic but expensive recovery purchases for required corn and pineapple planting stock so world-generation luck cannot hard-lock progression.
- Kept recovery routes intentionally costly so natural exploration remains the preferred acquisition path.

## Copper and Ironworking

- Copper is now a real technology era across Levels 7-9 instead of a brief prerequisite.
- Level 7 establishes copper supply, the complete Better Copper toolset, workshop supplies, and copper-era utility production.
- Ironworking is granted only by the Level 10 promotion.
- Added validation that prevents Level 7-9 from granting or bypassing the `iron_age` stage.

## Food processing

- Level 8 now genuinely requires the Farmer's Delight Cutting Board and flint knife.
- Chicken sandwiches use cooked chicken cuts instead of whole cooked chicken.
- Fish stew uses cooked cod and salmon slices instead of whole fish.
- Stuffed potatoes use beef patties so the cutting-board processor cannot be bypassed.
- Level 11 now requires a Slice and Dice Slicer and uses it as real progression infrastructure rather than a dead namespace unlock.

## Automation economy

- Level 9 remains the last intentionally hand-manageable production tier.
- Create arrives at Level 10, after which renewable Bountiful demand scales geometrically:
  - Level 10: 4x
  - Level 11: 8x
  - Level 12: 16x
  - Level 13: 32x
  - Level 14: 64x
  - Level 15: 128x
- Raised Bountiful reward caps so industrial orders can still pay proportionally at high throughput.
- Promotion currency ramps from 48 / 96 / 192 iron coins at Levels 10-12 to 48 / 96 / 192 gold coins at Levels 13-15, with increasingly large previous-economy goods alongside the currency.
- Late Market Orders now demand hundreds of renewable farm and prepared-food outputs, making automated farming, kitchens, storage, and logistics the practical solution.

## Infrastructure retention

- Level 12 now asks players to build and keep the electric motor, alternator, and accumulator instead of crafting expensive machines only to surrender them.
- Level 12's Market Order consumes renewable throughput from wheat, tacos, fried rice, and salmon rolls instead.
- Level 14 keeps the Cave Tablet as expedition infrastructure while consuming deep resources and industrial farm provisions.
- Level 15 keeps the Twilight Forest Magic Map as navigation infrastructure.
- Repeatable Bountiful objectives exclude motors, alternators, accumulators, Cave Tablets, and Magic Maps entirely.

## Late game integrations

- Level 13 establishes Alex's Delight wildlife cuisine while keeping the large throughput burden on automatable older cattle and corn production.
- Level 14 combines Alex's Caves deep resources with large-scale expedition provisioning instead of ending as a rare-item checklist.
- Integrated End's Delight into Level 15 as a renewable chorus-fruit-pie branch.
- Chorus fruit pie reconnects End chorus farming to older wheat, sugar, and Farmer's Delight pie crust.
- Level 15's Market Order consumes 128 chorus fruit pies alongside Twilight cuisine and hundreds of older prepared foods.
- The Level 15 repeatable Bountiful pool keeps chorus fruit pies in the 128x industrial market after the one-time order.

## Validation and release safety

- Expanded quest graph validation for unique IDs, dependency reachability, cycles, the 15-level Market Order spine, and deliberate branch structure.
- Added quest-description validation and quest-text normalization checks.
- Added automation-economy validation for onboarding, deterministic crop recovery, processor use, retained infrastructure, promotion costs, geometric bounty scaling, and peak Market Orders.
- Added retained-infrastructure validation so repeatable Bountiful orders cannot accidentally demand permanent enabling objects.
- CI continues to build and verify client/server archives and boot a dedicated Forge server with KubeJS runtime/data error detection.
