# Bounty Harvest 0.12.0

## 15-level farm economy overhaul

- Rebuilt Levels 1-8 around production chains instead of vanilla checklist filler.
- Added Levels 9-15 with Corn Delight, Create, Pineapple Delight, Slice & Dice, Create Crafts & Additions, Alex's Delight, Alex's Caves, Twilight Forest, and Twilight's Flavors & Delight progression.
- Added `PROGRESSION_DESIGN.md` as the design contract for future progression work: old production must keep gaining value, new levels should multiply existing systems, crop introductions should be paced, and technology tiers must have room to breathe.
- Expanded copper into a full Level 7-9 technology era. Completing the Better Copper toolset proves copper mastery but no longer grants Ironworking.
- Moved the single Ironworking unlock to the Level 10 promotion. Advancing into iron now consumes copper and charcoal from the mature pre-iron economy before exposing iron ore, ingots, buckets, tools, armor, and iron-dependent kitchen equipment.
- Reworked Level 7 around workshop supplies so charcoal, leather/paper/bookshelves, wool/yarn/sweaters, wood, and copper all return as useful production lines instead of becoming dead inventory.
- Staggered Farmer's Delight crops instead of introducing four crops at once: cabbage at Level 8, tomato at Level 9, onion at Level 10, and rice at Level 11.
- Rebuilt Level 8 as a pre-iron Market Garden & Hearth tier. Cabbage combines with older chicken, fish, potatoes, carrots, apples, wheat dough, eggs, and sugar through prepared foods before iron exists.
- Reworked Level 9 tacos into a cross-level product using corn/tortilla, cooked pork, tomato, and cabbage so the new crop economy increases the value of older animal and garden production.
- Reworked Level 10 into Iron & Industry: Ironworking, onion, the iron kitchen, Create foundations, and redstone now arrive together as the first major industrial breakthrough.
- Delayed rice to Level 11 so it immediately supports pineapple fried rice and reconnects the old fisheries economy through salmon rolls.
- Moved fisheries to Level 5 and restored the vanilla fishing-rod recipe so the tier is reachable before Ironworking.
- Integrated Aquaculture 2 and an actually early Ocean's Delight recipe instead of requiring late kitchen ingredients.
- Reworked every level-up into a market promotion paid with goods and the currency available from the preceding bounty tier.
- Added a Market Order milestone to every level; later contracts intentionally reuse earlier crops, animal goods, foods, textiles, fuel, and machinery.
- Rebuilt the repeatable decree market for Levels 1-15 with denomination-appropriate prices.
- Added readable high-priority KubeJS Bountiful data for all 15 decree/objective pools and copper/iron/gold reward tiers, including stronger processed reuse for chicken, fish, corn, rice, charcoal, leather, and textiles.
- Redistributed ItemStages gates across all 15 levels and removed the misleading Alex's Mobs namespace gate; entities are not represented as item-gated systems.
- Moved redstone to Level 10, gold to the electrical tier, and diamond/lapis to the deep-expedition tier.
- Extended quest/pack validation from 8 to 15 levels, including the market-order spine, generated bounty data, namespace policy, reachability, cycles, delayed Level 10 Ironworking, crop pacing, quest descriptions, and major old-production reuse invariants.
